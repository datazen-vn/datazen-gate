# Code of conduct

We want working here to be respectful and productive for everyone, people and agents alike.

## Expected behaviour

- Be courteous and assume good faith.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person; be specific and kind.
- Say plainly what you know and what you do not know.
- Respect other people's time and ownership of their area.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, discrimination or threats.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Pressuring people to skip checks, reviews or safety steps.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close discussions or restrict participation that break these rules. Report a problem to the maintainers listed in `CODEOWNERS`.
